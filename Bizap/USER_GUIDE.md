## Quick Start

### First Time Setup

1. **Open Bizap**
   - App launches with onboarding screen
   - Create your business profile
   - Enter business name and ABN

2. **Enter Your First Invoice**
   - Tap "New Invoice" button
   - Select a customer (or create new)
   - Add line items (description, qty, price)
   - Save invoice

3. **Send to Customer**
   - Invoice appears in "Invoices Sent"
   - Share PDF via email or messaging
   - Track when customer pays

### 💰 Invoices

#### Create Invoice

1. Tap "New Invoice" button
2. **Select Customer:** Choose existing or create new
3. **Add Line Items:**
   - Description (e.g., "Website Design")
   - Quantity (1, 2, 3...)
   - Unit Price ($100.00, $50.00...)
4. **Review Total:** App shows subtotal + total
5. **Due Date:** Set when payment is due
6. **Invoice Number:** Auto-generated (INV-001, INV-002...)
7. **Customize (Optional):**
   - Go to Settings → Invoice Settings
   - Change numbering prefix
   - Add header/footer text
   - Select payment methods to display
8. **Save:** Click "Save" in top-right
9. **Status Changes to "SENT"**

#### Track Payment

1. **View Invoice:** Tap from list
2. **See Status:**
   - Green checkmark = Paid
   - Orange = Partially paid
   - Red = Overdue
3. **Record Payment:**
   - Tap payment icon (💳)
   - Enter amount customer paid
   - Set payment date
   - Tap "Record Payment"
4. **Status Updates Automatically**

#### Send to Customer

1. Open invoice
2. Tap "Share" button
3. Choose method:
   - Email
   - Text message
   - Whatsapp
   - Copy link

#### Customize Invoices

1. Go to **Settings** → **Invoice Settings**
2. **Numbering:**
   - Prefix (e.g., "INV-" or "2024-")
   - Starting number
3. **Header/Footer:**
   - Add custom text
   - Payment method notes
4. **Layout Options:**
   - Show/hide notes section
   - Show/hide tax ID
   - Show company branding
5. **Save**

### 💳 Payments

#### Record Payment

**Option 1: From Invoice Detail**
1. Open invoice
2. Tap payment icon
3. Enter amount
4. Set date
5. Tap "Record Payment"

**Option 2: From Invoice List**
1. Long-press invoice
2. Tap "Record Payment"
3. Enter amount and date
4. Confirm

#### Payment Methods

When recording payment, specify method:
- **Cash**
- **Check**
- **ACH Transfer**
- **Wire Transfer**
- **Credit Card**
- **Debit Card**
- **Mobile Payment** (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- **Other**

#### View Payment History

1. Open invoice
2. Scroll down to "Payment History"
3. See all payments recorded
4. Tap payment for details

#### Refund a Payment

1. Open payment in history
2. Tap "Refund"
3. Enter refund amount
4. Confirm
5. Invoice status updates

### 📈 Analytics

#### Dashboard Metrics

On the home screen, see:
- **Unpaid:** Count of invoices awaiting payment
- **Overdue:** Count of past-due invoices
- **Paid This Month:** Count of invoices received payment

#### Payment Analytics

Go to **Settings → Payment Analytics** to see:

**Summary Cards:**
- Outstanding balance ($ owed)
- Collection rate (% of invoices paid)
- Days to payment (average)

**Charts:**
- Payment trends over time
- Invoices by status (pie chart)
- Top customers by payment

**Filters:**
- Filter by date range (start → end date)
- Filter by invoice status (Sent, Paid, Overdue)
- Export as CSV or PDF

#### Revenue Dashboard

Go to **Settings → Revenue Dashboard** to see:

**Metrics:**
- Expected Revenue (total invoiced)
- Actual Revenue (total paid)
- Outstanding (still owed)

**Charts:**
- Revenue by month
- Paid vs. Unpaid
- Top revenue customers

### ⚙️ Settings

#### Business Profile

Go to **Settings → Business Profile**

Edit:
- Business name
- ABN / Business ID
- Logo (upload business photo)
- Default currency
- Tax rate

#### Appearance

Go to **Settings → App Appearance**

Customize:
- **Theme:** Light, Dark, or Auto
- **Color Scheme:** 
  - Select preset (Blue, Green, Purple, etc.)
  - Or customize colors manually:
    - Primary (main brand color)
    - Secondary (accent color)
    - Tertiary (highlight color)
- All changes save automatically

#### Invoice Settings

Go to **Settings → Invoice Settings**

Customize:
- Invoice number prefix
- Starting invoice number
- Header text
- Footer text
- Payment method notes
- Show/hide sections

#### Backup & Restore

Go to **Settings → App Appearance → Backup & Restore**

**Backup:**
1. Tap "Backup Data"
2. Choose:
   - All Data
   - Customers Only
   - Invoices Only
3. App creates backup file
4. Download or share

**Restore:**
1. Tap "Restore Data"
2. Choose backup file
3. Confirm restore
4. All data restored

**Reset Data:**
1. Type "DELETE" when prompted
2. Confirm
3. ⚠️ This cannot be undone!

## FAQ

**Q: Can I edit an invoice after sending?**  
A: Yes! Open the invoice, tap "Edit", make changes, and save. Status stays the same.

**Q: What happens if I record a payment on the same day as creating the invoice?**  
A: That's fine! Bizap allows same-day payments. Just tap the payment icon and record it.

**Q: Can I delete a customer with invoices?**  
A: Yes, but all their invoices stay in the system for record-keeping.

**Q: How do I export data for my accountant?**  
A: Go to Settings → Backup & Restore → Export. Choose customers, invoices, or all data.

**Q: Does Bizap require internet?**  
A: No! Everything works offline. No internet = no problem.

**Q: Can I customize the invoice format?**  
A: Yes! Go to Settings → Invoice Settings to change numbering, header/footer, and layouts.

**Q: How do I back up my data?**  
A: Settings → Backup & Restore → Backup Data. Creates a file you can download.

**Q: What if I accidentally delete something?**  
A: You can restore from a backup. Without a backup, deleted data is permanent.

**Q: Can I see when a customer views my invoice?**  
A: Not with Bizap (basic tracking). You can see when they make a payment.

**Q: How do I change my business profile?**  
A: Settings → Business Profile. Edit and save changes.

## Tips & Tricks

### 1. Use the Dashboard Daily
Check metrics each morning to stay on top of payments.

### 2. Set Due Dates Carefully
- Net 7 = 7 days to pay
- Net 30 = 30 days to pay
- Clear expectations reduce payment delays

### 3. Follow Up on Overdue
- Dashboard shows overdue invoices
- Reach out to customer quickly
- Quick follow-up = faster payment

### 4. Organize Vault
- Use consistent file naming
- Keep invoices grouped by customer
- Clean up old files monthly

### 5. Regular Backups
- Backup weekly during active season
- Backup monthly during slow season
- Test restore to ensure backup works

### 6. Use Notes
- Add notes for special arrangements
- Track communication with customers
- Remember context for next time
